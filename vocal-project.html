The Vocal Project

Communication Competition Series

The Vocal Project is NSUCC's public speaking and communication competition that provides a platform for students to showcase their oratory skills. This annual event brings together talented speakers from various universities to compete in different categories of public communication, promoting excellence in verbal expression and confidence building.

Competition Categories

  • Impromptu Speaking - Spontaneous speech delivery on given topics
  • Prepared Speech - Pre-planned presentations on relevant themes
  • Debate Competition - Structured argumentation and counter-argumentation
  • Storytelling - Narrative presentation with creative elements
  • Panel Discussion - Group discussions on contemporary issues

Participating Universities

  • North South University (NSU)
  • BRAC University
  • Independent University Bangladesh (IUB)
  • American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB)
  • East West University
  • United International University (UIU)
  • Daffodil International University
  • University of Dhaka (Guest Participants)

Awards & Recognition

  • Champion - Trophy + Certificate + Prize
  • Runner-up - Trophy + Certificate + Prize
  • 2nd Runner-up - Trophy + Certificate + Prize
  • Best Speaker - Special Recognition Award
  • People's Choice - Audience Favorite Award
  • Participation Certificates - All qualified participants

Distinguished Judges Panel

  • Senior Faculty Members from leading universities
  • Media personalities and journalists
  • Corporate communication executives
  • Professional public speakers and trainers
  • Previous competition champions (alumni panel)
  • International guest judges (special editions)

Competition Format

  • Registration - Online application with CV and motivation letter
  • Preliminary Round - Initial screening and selection
  • Quarter Finals - 32 participants compete in themed rounds
  • Semi Finals - Top 8 participants advance
  • Grand Finale - Final 4 compete for championship
  • Award Ceremony - Recognition and networking session

What Participants Gain

  • Enhanced public speaking and presentation skills
  • Confidence building through competitive experience
  • Networking opportunities with peers and professionals
  • Media exposure and recognition
  • Constructive feedback from expert judges
  • Certificate of participation for CV enhancement
  • Potential scholarship and internship opportunities
