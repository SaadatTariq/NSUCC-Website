Communicado

Inter-University Communication Championship

Communicado is NSUCC's comprehensive inter-university communication competition featuring multiple categories of competitive events. This annual championship brings together students from various universities to compete in debate, public speaking, creative writing, and other communication-related activities, fostering academic excellence and inter-institutional collaboration.

Competition Categories

  • Debate Championship - British Parliamentary & Asian Parliamentary formats
  • Public Speaking - Impromptu and prepared speech competitions
  • Model United Nations (MUN) - Diplomatic simulation sessions
  • Creative Writing - Poetry, short stories, and essay competitions
  • Presentation Competition - Business case studies and project presentations
  • Quiz Competition - General knowledge and current affairs

Participating Universities

  • North South University (NSU) - Host University
  • BRAC University
  • Independent University Bangladesh (IUB)
  • American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB)
  • East West University
  • United International University (UIU)
  • University of Dhaka
  • Bangladesh University of Engineering & Technology (BUET)
  • Daffodil International University
  • Southeast University
  • Stamford University Bangladesh
  • University of Liberal Arts Bangladesh (ULAB)

Championship Awards

  • Overall Champion University - Champion Trophy + Prize
  • Runner-up University - Runner-up Trophy + Prize
  • Individual Category Winners - Trophies + Prizes
  • Individual Category Runners-up - Medals + Recognition
  • Best Speaker Awards - Special Recognition + Prizes
  • Participation Certificates - All registered participants

Esteemed Judges Panel

  • Senior University Faculty from Communications & English Departments
  • Media Industry Professionals and Journalists
  • Corporate Communication Executives
  • Diplomats and International Relations Experts
  • Professional Debate Coaches and Trainers
  • Alumni Champions from Previous Competitions

Competition Format & Timeline

  • Day 1: Registration, Opening Ceremony, Preliminary Rounds
  • Day 2: Quarter Finals, Semi Finals for all categories
  • Day 3: Final Rounds, Championship Decisions
  • Evening: Cultural Program, Award Ceremony, Closing
  • Format: Knockout system with cumulative university scoring
  • Duration: 3-day intensive competition weekend

Networking & Benefits

  • Inter-university networking opportunities with top communicators
  • Exposure to media coverage and social media promotion
  • Professional development workshops during breaks
  • Mentorship opportunities with industry professionals
  • Scholarship recommendations for outstanding performers
  • Alumni network access for future career opportunities
  • International competition recommendations for winners

Registration Process

  • University team registration through official communication clubs
  • Individual category registrations open to all students
  • Early bird registration discounts available
  • Registration includes meals, certificates, and competition materials
  • Limited slots available - first come, first served basis
  • Registration deadline: 2 weeks before competition date
