Job Column

Career Development Initiative

Job Column is NSUCC's career development initiative that connects NSU students and recent graduates with job opportunities and professional development resources. Through partnerships with various organizations and companies, the program provides career guidance, skill development workshops, and networking opportunities to help students transition from academic life to professional careers.

Platform Objectives

  • Connect students with relevant job opportunities
  • Build strategic industry partnerships for placements
  • Provide career counseling and guidance services
  • Facilitate professional networking opportunities
  • Support resume building and interview preparation
  • Bridge the gap between academia and industry

Partner Industries

  • Media & Communications - TV channels, newspapers, digital media
  • Corporate Communications - MNCs, local corporations, agencies
  • Digital Marketing - Social media agencies, content companies
  • Education - Schools, universities, training institutes
  • Non-Profit - NGOs, development organizations
  • Technology - IT companies, startups, tech firms

Program Impact

  • Successful job placements for program participants
  • Strong network of industry partnerships and connections
  • Active alumni network supporting career development
  • Regular career guidance and mentorship sessions
  • Professional development workshops and training
  • Industry networking events and job fairs

Services Offered

  • Personalized job matching based on skills and interests
  • Resume review and optimization services
  • Mock interview sessions with industry professionals
  • Career counseling and pathway guidance
  • Industry networking events and job fairs
  • Professional development workshops and seminars
  • LinkedIn profile optimization and personal branding
  • Follow-up support during probation periods

How to Get Started

  • Submit your CV and career preferences
  • Attend our career counseling session
  • Complete skills assessment and profile building
  • Receive matched job opportunities
  • Get interview preparation support
  • Secure your ideal career placement
