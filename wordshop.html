Wordshop

Educational Workshop Series

Wordshop is NSUCC's flagship annual interactive event that educates its audience on turning creative inspiration into ambition. This comprehensive workshop-based session provides valuable insights on transforming hobbies or passions into viable career paths, connecting NSU students directly with industry experts and successful professionals from various fields.

Workshop Objectives

  • Transform creative inspiration into professional ambition
  • Connect students with industry experts and mentors
  • Provide practical insights for career development
  • Bridge the gap between passion and profession
  • Offer hands-on learning experiences

Past Workshop Topics

  • Fiction Writing - Led by NSU EML Faculty
  • Poetry & Creative Expression - The Versemongers
  • Journalism & Cartooning - Colours Magazine Team
  • Teaching & Education - Teach for Bangladesh
  • Content Creation & Digital Media - Sadman Sadik, Enayet Chowdhury
  • Startups & Entrepreneurship - Shikho & Pathao Co-founders

What Participants Gain

  • Direct mentorship from industry professionals
  • Practical career guidance and insights
  • Networking opportunities with experts
  • Hands-on workshop experiences
  • Certificate of participation
  • Career pathway recommendations

Event Format

  • Full-day interactive workshop sessions
  • Multiple industry expert presentations
  • Q&A sessions with professionals
  • Hands-on activities and exercises
  • Networking opportunities during breaks
  • Resource sharing and career guidance
