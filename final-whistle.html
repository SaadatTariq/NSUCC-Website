ICCB Intra-Club Football Tournament 2025

🏆 ICCB presents: The Final Whistle Intra-Club Football Tournament 2025 ⚽

The Final Whistle is the club's premier sports and recreation event series, designed to blend competitive activities with community storytelling. The series kicked off its major internal competition with the highly successful ICCB Intra-Club Football Tournament in 2025.

The Inaugural Championship: A Strategy Game

The 2025 tournament introduced a unique, strategic element that captivated participants: The Player Auction.

  • Title Sponsor: ICCB (Integrated into the official event name)
  • Format: Intra-Club 7-a-side Football Tournament
  • Team Ownership: Teams were managed and owned by the club's Executive Board members
  • The Auction: Owners were given a specific credit budget to bid for players from the club roster, ensuring balanced competition and strategic team building
